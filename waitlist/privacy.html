What we collect on the waitlist

  • Your email address
  • Your phone type (iPhone or Android), country and city
  • Whether you are a woman or a man, and who you want to meet
  • Your browser language, the link you came from (for example "tiktok"), and when you signed up and confirmed

Why

To email you when Popdate launches, and to plan where and on which phones to launch first. Popdate rooms need people who want to meet each other, so we look at the balance between groups in each city. We do not sell your data or use it for advertising.

Legal basis

Your consent, which you give by ticking the box (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR; Art. 6 and 31 Swiss FADP). You can withdraw it anytime with the link in our email or by writing to us.

Who processes it for us

  • Convex, Inc. (USA): stores the waitlist
  • Vercel Inc. (USA): hosts this website
  • Resend, Inc. (USA): sends the confirmation and launch emails

These providers may process data in the USA. We use them under data processing agreements with the EU Standard Contractual Clauses or the EU–US Data Privacy Framework.

How long we keep it

Until we have sent the launch email and for at most 6 months after launch, or until you leave the waitlist, whichever comes first. Unconfirmed signups are deleted after 30 days.

Your rights

You can ask what we store about you, have it corrected or deleted, receive it in a common format, and object to its use. Every email has a link that deletes your details right away. You can also complain to your data protection authority, in Switzerland the FDPIC.
